2. reproduce the blocker from repository evidence;

sufficiency conclusion. An approved blocked report does not authorize its missing
dependency. Preserve the active assignment if no supported scope-update mechanism
is available; do not invent an outcome or reimport the sprint.

Do not claim independent review or Formal GO.

First produce a source-bound prerequisite checkpoint within two active hours of
the authorized investigation budget. This is not a theorem completion estimate.
Record the exact required statement and PO-AB1 link; distinguish proved results,

whether R2.3, new source/provenance binding or model changes are needed; list a

Extended proof work is permitted only after both reviews and Coordinator confirm

existing `RESUME_FORMAL` route. Amendment reviews are not checkpoint reviews;
review approval of an accurately blocked report is not permission for its excluded
dependency. Once these conditions hold, necessary lemmas need no separate user
permission. A materially revised checkpoint needs two reviews of its new commit.

theorem registration and axiom audit, pinned environment preparation, and new

binding, native admission, production Init/Next, certificate/WAL semantics or new
trust authority. Do not assume the missing dependency to obtain PASS. Production
recovery, other R3, protocol/model changes, deployment, guard removal and gate
weakening remain excluded. The known FAIL permits only repair of its named cause,
not integration. After discharge and required checks, return to original D01

nor Formal GO. Return `GO`, `NO_GO`, `NEED_DECISION`, or `STOP` as applicable.
