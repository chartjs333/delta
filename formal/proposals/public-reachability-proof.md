# Reachable public machine and all-vote replay invariant

Candidate amendment 0001; T044/T048/T049/T057/T060. This stage adds
`PublicReachability.lean` and `PublicReachabilityExamples.lean` to the mandatory
Lean project. Thirty general helper theorems establish one invariant across
successful executable operations. Thirty example/composition theorems include
two generic fixture-sequence helpers, four kernel-decide crash/restart facts,
composed reachable histories, and five unreachable-state counterchecks.
All sixty declarations are axiom-audited. This is not the full
`nativeArithmeticRecoveryRefines` obligation; mandatory coverage remains 44/45.

## What is derived

`Reachable` begins at `PublicRecovery.initial actor current`, with an empty
all-vote journal and no sent or pending slot. Each transition constructor
requires the result of an actual `persist`, `otherVote`, `advance`, `crash`,
`restart`, `recover`, or `exposeRetry` call. It accepts no claimed invariant,
recovered-state equality, prefix equality or arithmetic-admission flag.

Induction establishes that replaying the machine's entire ordered log from its
original initial journal produces exactly its current journal. Equality covers
the actor, every original slot and native record, and all three current pointers.
The replay append lemma composes actual checked steps, including non-arithmetic
votes and checked ApplyQC current advances. The initial journal remains exactly
the original actor/current with empty slots throughout the history.

The resulting slot list equals `voteSlots log`, preserving the order and original
sequence of all intervening votes. Every stored slot has a checked admission in
the derived history. For arithmetic slots, `reachableArithmeticPreparation`
extracts the independently resolved graph and original native preparation,
complete record, exact receipt/effect, and all-vote sequence allocation. It does
not accept a prevalidated arithmetic Boolean. Non-arithmetic authorization stays
the separately named `otherAuthorized` premise from PublicJournal.

An unresolved pending candidate has a checked admission against the unchanged
known journal and cannot coexist with ready mode. Crash/restart preserve that
candidate. Only exact authenticated presence or explicit verified absence can
resolve it. The restoration proof derives that the selected scan extends the
known log, composes actual replay and preserves every previously stored/sent
record. Incomplete, corrupt and ambiguous scans retain the known log; blocked
states cannot silently become ready. The candidate itself is not proof of a
durable write, and a missing response is not proof of absence.

Every locally exposed arithmetic slot belongs to the stored journal and has an
actual native record. This follows separately for first exposure and historical
retry, including retry after current advancement. Retry does not append or
recompute the old parent. Recovery preserves the sent set and returns no receipt;
its subsequent retry can return the original receipt. `sent` denotes local
exposure only, not authenticated network delivery or quorum voting power.

## Concrete composition and counterchecks

The examples compose the existing computed four-vote/eight-vote prefixes with
actual operations. They prove reachability through known unexposed persistence,
crash, restart, complete recovery and exact receipt retry; unknown persistence,
verified absence or presence; incomplete scans and corrupt blocking; and current
advance followed by historical retry. Original arithmetic sequences remain
5/6/8. No full public trace is renumbered.

Five negative proofs reject a ready machine with an unresolved pending slot, an
exposed receipt absent from the journal, a non-arithmetic slot with an invented
native receipt, a journal whose log was deleted, and a rewritten nonempty initial
journal. These follow from the general invariant, not supplied equalities.

The examples reuse fourteen previously validated public/native fixtures and
their existing finite codecs/hash samples and synthetic metadata/QC/scan trust.
No new public/native fixture is claimed. In particular UNKNOWN-to-presence and
explicit incomplete scan remain separately labelled mathematical cases. The new
composition file is hand-authored proof source, not a generated byte fixture.

## Remaining boundary

The environment/resolver is fixed throughout a reachable history. Availability
changes, arbitrary admission failures, arbitrary initial snapshots and repair
are outside this layer. Initial actor/current authentication and scan provenance
are assumptions, not consequences of the empty-journal construction. Rejected
calls are not new successful transitions in this relation.

The next obligation is binding this reachable relation to independently
authenticated original public event snapshots and canonical public state-root
transitions, non-arithmetic phase/QC admission and global SendVoteEnvelope/QC
eligibility. General SHA-256/decoding bounds, production exporter, physical
WAL/barrier/scan execution, clean offline reproduction, contract freeze and
independent reviews remain required. No TLA model, public schema, runtime guard,
production protocol transition, or accepted authority is changed by this stage.

Evidence is retained in `formal/proposals/evidence/public-reachability.json`.
Self-review is not independent attestation. Formal authority remains NO_GO.
